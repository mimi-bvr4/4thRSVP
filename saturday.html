The All-American 250th
Saturday, July 4th ★ 7:00 PM til the fireworks are over
The Bridge Building ★ Nashville
⏱️ Be downtown & inside before 8:00 PM

The Bridge Building sits inside the official fall zone for Nashville's fireworks. At 8:00 PM the fire marshal secures the entire site — and that includes us. If you're not inside by 8:00, you won't be able to get in. Plan to arrive with time to spare.

🍖 Dinner & drinks

Dinner is served by Martin's Bar-B-Que Joint. Open bar all night.

🚌 Parking & how to get there

Park in the gated lot under the Korean Veterans Bridge — an attendant will direct you. From the lot, walk down the riverfront to The Bridge Building.

From I-65, merge onto I-24 toward downtown, then follow the red route on the map below — onto Davidson St, then S. 1st St — to the gated lot under the Korean Veterans Bridge (marked ✕). Park before 8 PM, then walk the riverfront to the building.
